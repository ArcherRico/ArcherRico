About Me

My name is Enrique Johnson, I'm 25 years old, I love collecting action figures and spending time with my 2 cats, Bandit and Kubo. I worked at LEGO for 5 years straight out of high school and worked my way up to full-time supervisor. LEGO has always been a passion for me ever since I was a kid, so working there was a dream come true. In April of 2025, I decided to go back to school and pursue a college education, and I had to leave LEGO behind. I am currently studying Computer Programming at Saint Paul College in St. Paul, Minnesota. I made this website as a portfolio of my skills in developing websites.
